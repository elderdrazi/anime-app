# Kumo privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Kumo is an app for keeping a list of the anime you watch and sharing it with friends. This page says what Kumo stores about you, who can see it, and how to remove it.

Contact: elderdrazi@gmail.com

## What Kumo stores

| What | When | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Your username | When you create an account | Anyone using Kumo |
| Your list: shows, episodes watched, ratings | When you add or update a show | Anyone using Kumo who opens your profile, except people you've blocked |
| Your level, achievements and chosen picture border | As you use the app | Anyone using Kumo who opens your profile |
| Your profile picture | Only if you set one | Anyone using Kumo |
| Your friends list | When you add friends | Anyone using Kumo who opens your profile |
| Notes you send with a recommendation or a watch-together request | When you send one | The friend you send it to |
| Your email address and password | Only if you add an email | Nobody. The email is used for sign-in only and the password is never readable by anyone, including us |
| The list of people you've blocked | When you block someone | Only you |
| Reports you file about other people | When you file one | The people who run Kumo |
| Counters for streaks and recommendations | As you use the app | Only you; they feed your achievements |

Kumo does not collect your location, contacts, phone number, advertising ID or anything about your other apps. It has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Your profile picture is chosen through Android's photo picker, which hands Kumo only the one picture you pick. Kumo never has access to the rest of your photos.

## Where it's stored

Your account and everything in the table above are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a service run by Google. Google processes this data on our behalf. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Other services Kumo talks to

- **AniList.** Show titles, artwork, descriptions and scores come from [AniList](https://anilist.co). Your searches are sent to AniList to get results. If you connect an AniList account, Kumo sends that AniList username to AniList to read its public list. The AniList username is kept on your phone only.
- **GitHub.** The version of Kumo installed from GitHub checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from there. The Google Play version doesn't.

These services see your device's internet address, as any website you visit does. Kumo sends them nothing else about you.

## How long it's kept

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove a show, a rating or your picture at any time in the app, and you can delete your whole account.

## Deleting your account

In the app: **Profile → Delete account**. This removes your list, ratings, achievements, picture, friends, inbox and username, and deletes your sign-in.

If you can't use the app, see [how to delete your account](delete-account.md) or email elderdrazi@gmail.com from the address on the account.

Notes you sent to friends stay in their inbox until they remove them. Reports you filed are kept so they can be acted on.

## Children

Kumo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we'll remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
