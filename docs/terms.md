## What you can't post

Your username, profile picture and the notes you send to friends must not contain:

- Sexual content or nudity
- Hate speech, threats, bullying or harassment
- Anything illegal, or that infringes someone else's rights
- Spam or advertising

## Reporting and blocking

- To report a person, open their profile, tap the menu in the corner and choose **Report**. Reports are reviewed by the people who run Kumo.
- To stop someone seeing your list or contacting you, choose **Block** from the same menu.
