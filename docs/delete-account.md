# Deleting your Kumo account

App: **Kumo** (com.kumo.anime)

## In the app

1. Open Kumo and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
3. If you added an email, type your password to confirm.
4. Tap **Delete forever**.

## Without the app

Email **elderdrazi@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my Kumo account". Send it from the email address attached to the account and include your username. Accounts with no email attached can only be deleted from the phone they were created on, because there's no other way to prove who owns them. We delete accounts within 30 days of a request.

## What's deleted

- Your username, which becomes free for someone else to use
- Your list, episode progress and ratings
- Your level and achievements
- Your profile picture and picture border
- Your friends list, and your entry on each friend's list
- Your inbox, pending friend requests and watch-together requests
- Your list of blocked people
- Your sign-in, including your email address and password

## What's kept

- Notes you sent to friends stay in their inbox until they remove them.
- Reports you filed about other people are kept so they can be acted on.

Nothing else is retained.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
